ESP32 Weather Station: Design Document

A battery-powered outdoor weather station that measures temperature, humidity and pressure and reports them over Wi-Fi.

Document: WS-DOC-001 Version: 0.1 (draft) Author: Your Name Date: 2 October 2026 Hardware: Rev A
  1. Overview
  2. Requirements
  3. Design
  4. Build record
  5. Verification summary
  6. Open issues and revisions

1. Overview

Purpose. Provide a low-cost outdoor weather station that runs for a year on one battery and sends readings to a home server.

In scope. Sensor, microcontroller, power supply, firmware and enclosure.

Out of scope. The server, dashboard and mounting hardware.

2. Requirements

Priority: M = must have, S = should have. Status shows whether the current design meets the requirement.

2.1 Functional

IDRequirementPri.Status
FR-01The unit shall measure temperature, relative humidity and air pressure.MMet
FR-02The unit shall send a reading to the server every 10 minutes over Wi-Fi.MMet
FR-03The unit shall retry and then sleep if Wi-Fi is unavailable.MOpen
FR-04The unit shall report its battery voltage with each reading.SOpen

2.2 Performance

IDRequirementPri.Status
PR-01Temperature range -20 to +50 °C, accuracy ±0.5 °C.MOpen
PR-02Relative humidity accuracy ±3 %.MOpen
PR-03Pressure accuracy ±1 hPa.MOpen
PR-04Battery life of at least 12 months on a 3000 mAh cell (average current below 0.34 mA).MOpen

2.3 Environmental and mechanical

IDRequirementPri.Status
ER-01The unit shall operate outdoors at -20 to +50 °C.MOpen
ER-02The enclosure shall protect against rain (IP54 or better) while venting the sensor.MOpen
ER-03The battery shall be rechargeable over USB without opening the enclosure.SOpen

2.4 Constraints

IDRequirementPri.Status
CR-01Bill of materials shall cost under £25.MMet
CR-02All parts shall be commonly available, with no custom PCB in Rev A.SMet

3.3 Firmware

Each cycle runs: wake, read sensor, read battery voltage, connect to Wi-Fi, send, then deep sleep for 10 minutes.

ModuleResponsibility
sensorRead the BME280 and return values in engineering units
netConnect to Wi-Fi, send a reading, retry on failure with a timeout
powerMeasure battery voltage and enter deep sleep

3.4 Power budget

Target average current is below 0.34 mA (PR-04). The first estimate fails, so the design uses a faster Wi-Fi connection.

StateCurrentTime per cycleCharge per cycle
Active (first estimate)120 mA3.0 s360 mAs
Deep sleep10 µA597 s6 mAs
Average, first estimate366 mAs / 600 s = 0.61 mA (does not meet PR-04)
Active (revised)80 mA0.5 s40 mAs
Average, revised46 mAs / 600 s = 0.077 mA (meets PR-04)

3.5 Design decisions

DecisionAlternativesReason
BME280 sensorDHT22, SHT31Measures all three quantities, low sleep current, cheap
ESP32 with deep sleepESP8266, LoRa nodeBuilt-in Wi-Fi and a good sleep mode, no gateway needed
Static IP and stored channelDHCP each wakeCuts Wi-Fi connect time and so the active charge
